# Five-target TP73/CUT&RUN promoter reports

This bundle contains the tall integrated locus reports for **CD44, TGFB1,
SERPINE1, PATZ1, and TP73**, generated by producer revision
`2bbb906e82a504ea231a0859b85f60a5a3d6fa7e` from the prepared Human GRCh38
Ensembl 116 reference.

Each report contains:

- three explicitly selected reporter transcripts and their architectures;
- Ensembl regulatory annotation, with exact-coordinate eMAR duplicates folded
  into the matching visible feature while all source rows remain in JSON;
- 12 TP73 CUT&RUN/H3K4me3 lanes, validated against native BigWig intervals;
- 30 accession-bound JASPAR 2026 tracks representing 28 factors, including
  separate MA0524.3, MA0814.3, and MA0815.1 TFAP2C tracks;
- translucent, colour-linked transcript-oriented −500/+200 TSS stretches; and
- promoterome similarity for every overlapping Ensembl regulatory feature.

The five corresponding FASTA files are retained in
`../target_tss_fastas/bundle/`. They contain every distinct annotated TSS for
each gene, not only the three transcripts selected for reporter presentation.
Every sequence is 701 bp and transcript-oriented.

## Validation summary

- 13 CUT&RUN-supported report-selected TSS windows;
- 8 connected −500/+200 stretches;
- 26 overlapping Ensembl-feature queries (4 below the 40-bp search gate);
- BLASTN replay against 389,722 transcript-linked promoter windows;
- 60/60 nonempty CUT&RUN/H3K4me3 lanes and 35,211 exact rendered intervals:
  CD44 4,354; TGFB1 6,136; SERPINE1 1,307; PATZ1 4,957; TP73 18,457.

The raw BLAST hit/match tables are regenerable and intentionally omitted; their
hashes and search limits remain in `comparison.json`. Absence of a qualifying
match is not a uniqueness claim. TFBS scores are sequence predictions and
CUT&RUN is occupancy/enrichment evidence; neither proves reporter activity,
direct regulation, biochemical affinity, or sufficiency.

`SHA256SUMS` binds every retained artifact in this directory. Per-report
receipts additionally bind the source report/SVG, candidate set, comparison,
renderer executable/version, and SVG/PDF/PNG outputs.
